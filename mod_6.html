How to create a stir in the market

When she was six Amanda Hamilton invited her friends to a mini sports day in her garden. 1. H I made a nice little profit, but then all the mothers rang mine to complain and I had to give the money back, she recalls.

Hamilton is the co-founder of Tea UK, a company that introduced a decent early-morning cup of tea to commuters. 'I found that getting a good cup of tea in London was a real struggle,' she says. 2 [Drag Here] Hamilton decided to grab the opportunity by founding her own company.

Discovering that the costs of establishing a tea café in London were prohibitive, she opted for a mobile tea-bar service. 3 [Drag Here] After two months of hunting, a site eventually arose at Lewisham railway station.

She started serving chai latte, a spice-based tea from India. 4 [Drag Here] So, in March 2002, she flew to the US to discover just how popular it was.

Realising it could take off in the UK, she decided to create her own version. 5 [Drag Here] 'The day they finally announced they preferred my chai latte to the imported version was divine,' says Hamilton.

Grammar Workshop: Expressing Purpose

Core Patterns:

  • to + infinitive
    We go to festivals to give people samples.
  • in order to + infinitive
    We had a stand in order to attract buyers.
  • so that + sentence
    Have a website so that you can sell internationally.
