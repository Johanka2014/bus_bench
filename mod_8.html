Grammar: The First Conditional

"If we buy the scales at €100 each, our mark-up won't allow us to make a profit."

Used for real possibilities in the present or future. If + present, will/can/might + infinitive.

Final Role-play: Negotiating the Deal

The Scenario: CorkPops

Buyers want 2,000 units at €8 with 60 days payment. Sellers want €12 with immediate payment.

  • Establish a relationship first (The Weather, The Hotel).
  • Discuss technical specs (The Needle, The Cartridge).
  • Move to terms: Price vs. Discount vs. Payment terms.

Key Phrases to Use:

"If we buy 500, could you give us an extra discount?"

"30 days is our bottom line, otherwise we'll have cashflow problems."

"How quickly could you supply them if they sell well?"
