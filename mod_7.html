Unit 7: A stand at a trade fair

Mastering the language and logistics of business exhibitions, from booking space to designing your brand impact.

Getting Started

Look at the images of trade fairs. Discuss these questions with a partner:

  • 1

    What is happening in the pictures? Describe the interactions between staff and visitors.

  • 2

    How can companies promote their products effectively by having a stand? Think about prestige, competitors, and handling products.

Case Study: Pop-the-Cork Ltd

Frank O'Donnell receives a memo and email from his boss, Bill Cowley, about next year's International Food Exhibition (IFE).

INTERNAL MEMO

From: Bill Cowley

To: All Staff

Subject: The International Food Exhibition

Following conversations with suppliers, I have decided that next year we will take a stand at the IFE in London. The objective will be to expand our customer base and find new export markets.

EMAIL

To: Frank O'Donnell

Subject: Action: IFE Organisers

Frank, can you get in touch with the organisers and find out:

  • How much it costs to have a stand.
  • When we need to book the space.

Final Challenge: The Meeting

You are Frank and Bill. You've received designs from 'Stand the Pace'. Decide on the following points for your reply:

Space

Decide exactly how much floor space (e.g. 40 sqm) you want to book.

Layout

Do you want an open stand (welcoming) or a closed stand (exclusive)?

Capacity

How many sales staff and visitors do you need to accommodate at once?

"We would like to book a corner site of 40 square metres and we'd prefer the design to be open..."
