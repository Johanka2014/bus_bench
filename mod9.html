Grammar: Tenses in Time Clauses

The Rule

For clauses starting with after, as soon as, before, until, while, when:

  • Talking about future: Use the PRESENT tense.
    "We'll discuss it when we MEET in June."
  • Talking about past/present: Use the same tense as the main clause.
  • With 'since': Use present perfect in main, past in time clause.
1. She'll call you as soon as she (come) out of the meeting.
2. We (be) happy since we (decide) to go it alone.
3. They didn't change premises until they (find) somewhere cheaper.
