JOB NOTES

Phone-answering Tips to Win Business

Part A: First Impressions

  • Answer all calls before the third ring.
  • Be warm and enthusiastic. Your voice is the company's image.
  • Welcome callers courteously and identify yourself and the company. e.g., "Cypress Technologies. Susan speaking."
  • Speak slowly and clearly with moderate volume.
  • Ask before putting someone on hold. Give progress reports every 30-45 seconds.

Part B: Professionalism

  • Use positive vocabulary. Instead of "I don't know", say "Let me find out".
  • Take messages completely and accurately. Ask for spelling if needed.
  • Respond to calls within one business day. Early response gets the contract!
  • Update your answering machine message regularly (e.g., for holidays).
  • Train everyone to answer the phone in the same professional manner.
